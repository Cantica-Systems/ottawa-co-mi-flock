Overview

The Holland Department of Public Safety uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising individual privacy. The Holland Department of Public Safety utilizes retroactive searches to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, the Holland Department of Public Safety utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, we have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Featured Success Stories

Homicide & Missing Endangered

2023- FSC used to track suspect and witness movements the night prior. Helped to verify witness' story and add credibility to it. Also helped build a timeline for the incident which is used in Homicide and high-level incidents.

2024- Endangered was diagnosed with Alzheimer and Diabetes. Endangered takes insulin every morning at 0900 hours which he does not have with him. Endangered likely left the residence at 0300 hours in MI/----. Multiple FLOCK hits with endangered being in Wyoming, MI at 0501 hours. Endangered subject was located by surveillance units utilizing FLOCK to narrow down endangered location

2024- Female was entered into LEIN as Missing Endangered. Endangered left Holland around 1330 hours and was enroute to Inkster, Michigan to visit family. Endangered was driving a vehicle which was plated and a description of the vehicle was given to officers. Officers utilized this information to search FLOCK cameras and managed to obtain a match to the endangered vehicle in Dearborn. Detectives used this information to coordinate with other agencies in Michigan to locate the subject who was not harmed.

2025- Assisted SSCENT, a northern MSP narcotics team in locating their fugitive who had threatened to kill a detective once the suspect learned he had sold narcotics to an undercover detective. SSCENT advised that the suspect was possibly in the Muskegon area but working in Ottawa County. WEMET utilized FSC and established a pattern of life locating the suspect while on his way to work at Tyson foods. OTSH Deputies assisted taking the suspect into custody.

2026- WEMET detectives were working an extensive narcotics investigation in the Holland area. Numerous arrests were made throughout the investigation, but one individual was outstanding and was difficult to locate. WEMET received a tip on a possible boyfriend of the suspect. FSC was used to locate the vehicle establishing a pattern of life that led detectives to a new apartment, not known to detectives. During the investigation the suspect fugitive was seen outside the apartment and was taken into custody.
